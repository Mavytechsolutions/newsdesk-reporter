⚙ One-time setup — YouTube Stream URLs
  1. Open youtube.com/livestreaming → Stream.
  2. Select stream key → Create new stream key → protocol HLS → save.
  3. Copy the Stream URL (starts with https) and paste it above. It contains your key — keep it secret.
  4. For Shorts make a second key the same way and paste it in the Shorts box.
  5. Always start the studio with the Newsdesk Studio app — it opens the editor and the live window together.

How to use

Every day — one news story in 6 steps

  1. + New → press Use on your ★ favorite (or pick a size: 16:9 YouTube/TV, 9:16 Reels/Shorts).
  2. Media → Import all of today's videos and photos at once (hold Ctrl to pick many). They stay in the library. Click the ones you need in order and press Add to timeline — they play one after another.
  3. Text → type the breaking headline, ticker lines and story. Add a Name strap when a reporter or guest speaks.
  4. Audio → paste the script, Generate the female voice-over. Select the video and turn on Noise cancellation if the field sound is noisy.
  5. Optional Extras → a full-screen Transition card, Effects or an EXCLUSIVE sticker.
  6. Export → 1080p, 25 fps → upload the MP4.

Under the player▶ play · ✂ cut at the playhead (splits one video into two windows in the Two windows template) · 🔈 mute the video · 16:9 change size · ⛶ full screen.
Right panelClick anything on the player or timeline to edit it there: text, colours, place on screen, timing, sound, noise cancellation.
TimelineDrag the bar above the timeline up (or press ⇕ Taller) to see more tracks. All clips of one screen sit on one row. Drag a clip onto another to change the order — the rest move along. Drag the ends to trim. 👁 hides a row, 🔈 mutes it.
EndingThe video ends when the last video, picture or text slide/story page ends. Music, graphics and cards stop there too, and the music fades out.
Box transitionsSelect any box › Box transition: Flip, Wipe, Fade or Cut. Top Story › + Add story page flips the box to the next story.
Reporters & logins📥 Reporters (top right). 👥 Team & logins (admin only): add a person as Reporter, Editor or Admin, then 📤 Share on WhatsApp. Reporters log in on their phone and send breaking news, videos with headlines, or ask to go live. Everything waits in the 📥 Inbox until an admin or editor presses ▶ Put on air — unless you switch on trusted for that person.
Go Live (YouTube)One button: 🔴 Go Live (top right). First time: paste your YouTube Stream URL in One-time setup. Then Start live. While live the same button becomes ⇪ Update live — press it to send your latest edits on air. No OBS needed.
Election resultsSwitch on Show results beside my video / photos (or use Anantapur municipal + visuals) to play your visuals next to the results. Results tab › type the parties, seats won and leading, vote % and each constituency's candidates. Every result screen (seat tally, seat arc, vote share, constituency) updates. Ready looks: Templates › Election results. For exit polls always fill in the Source.
Text only newsTemplates › Text only — full-screen text slides, one news item each, with transitions. Add more with Text › Text slide; type headlines in the Text tab.
Two windowsIn Media, pick Add to screen: Left / Right (or click a window on the player), then press + on the video for that screen. When one screen has no clip, the other grows to fill both — no empty window.
New project+ New empties the media library for the new story. Your files on the PC are not touched.
