Privacy Policy — MAVY News Maker

Last updated: 20 November 2026 · MAVYTECH SOLUTIONS PVT LTD, Anantapur, Andhra Pradesh, India

MAVY News Maker ("the app") lets reporters send news, pictures and videos to their own news studio, go live from the phone camera, and make news videos on the phone. This policy explains what the app uses and why.

What the app uses

  • Camera and microphone — only when you press Go LIVE or record, to send your live picture and sound to your studio.
  • Videos and pictures you choose — only the files you pick, to send them to your studio or to make a video on your phone.
  • Your login (User ID and password) — given to you by your studio, used only to connect you to that studio. It is kept on your phone.
  • Storage — finished videos are saved to your phone's Gallery (Movies/Newsdesk) when you make them.

Where your content goes

News, pictures, videos and live camera are sent directly from your phone to your own studio's computer using an encrypted peer-to-peer connection (WebRTC). A public connection service only helps the two devices find each other; it does not store your content. We (MAVYTECH SOLUTIONS) do not receive, store or sell your news, videos, pictures, location, contacts or personal data.

When you choose to share a video (for example to WhatsApp or YouTube), it is handled by the app you choose and by its own privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no tracking, no analytics, no selling of data.
  • No access to your contacts, messages, location or other files.

Children

The app is meant for news reporters and media teams, not for children under 13.

Deleting your data

Log out in the app and uninstall it to remove everything it kept on your phone. Your studio decides what happens to news you already sent to it.
